Key Takeaways · what to remember

Three sentences to remember

If a Joy team member can only remember three things from this map, these are them.

  • 01

    Joy is Unconventional, not new

    High distinctiveness (8.5), modest centrality (3.0). 21 months in market, 408 reviews, 5K+ merchants. The position is real and earned, not aspirational.

  • 02

    Climb to Aspirational via the X-axis

    Don't change the position — amplify it. Review velocity, industry-article share-of-voice, and Recharge-refugee narrative are the three accelerators on Centrality.

  • 03

    Loop is the closest neighbor — different lane

    Both Unconventional, but Loop owns mid-enterprise retention (642 reviews, 57% Plus); Joy owns Boutique-Players guided growth. Don't cross lanes.

D. Profit Formula

Synthesizing A (positioning), B (canvas), and C (operations) into three actions: what Joy should raise, what Joy should maintain, and what Joy should eliminate or reduce to compound profitability. Each item references the source analysis it came from.

↑
Raise
Invest more
  • Review velocity Push 408 → 1,000+ reviews in 12mo. A's biggest gap (centrality 3.0); B2's "social proof" is the leading signal merchants check. A · B2
  • Migration tooling for Recharge refugees Concierge migration team + automated import. A names Recharge decline (−5,514/90d) as Joy's biggest tailwind; B2 shows migration is Joy's weakest VoC factor (4 vs Loop 9). A · B2
  • Industry-article share-of-voice Get into the default top-7 list everywhere ("best Shopify subscription app" articles). A's Centrality climb depends on appearing in the buyer's consideration set. A
  • Cross-sell motion to Joy Loyalty / AOV / Chatty Productize the ecosystem hand-off. C identifies "ecosystem flywheel" as Joy's highest-leverage CAC reduction — turn it from happy accident into a sales playbook. C
  • International expansion (SEA / APAC / MENA / LATAM) Localize messaging + payment playbooks for non-US markets. A says Joy's centrality in non-US is already above the global average; this is the underserved segment competitors (84–97% US-merchants) ignore. A
  • Systematize the named-partner playbook Turn manual high-touch into a documented onboarding ops layer. C's geo-arbitrage formula only compounds if support quality scales without per-merchant cost growing linearly. C

Maintain
Keep as-is
  • "We only earn when you earn" pricing Core wedge defining Joy. A's biggest distinctiveness lever (8.5); B2 confirms "value for money" is a top-3 review theme. A · B2
  • Free 6 months / $1M revenue gateway Removes the primary objection from Boutique Players. C confirms it's the funnel converter that makes the entire formula work. C
  • All-features-day-1 (no upsell walls) C operation formula #3. B2 review theme: merchants explicitly praise "free unrestricted." Don't introduce gating — it would collapse the trust narrative. B2 · C
  • 24/7 live chat quality B2 puts Joy at top on support speed (9). Maintain depth even as volume grows — quality is the compound interest engine. B2
  • Done-for-you setup with named partners A's secondary distinctive pillar. B2's personal-touch score (9) is reviewer-confirmed; reviews name Avery, Philip, Olivia, Daniel, Iris. A · B2

Eliminate / Reduce
Stop or scale back
  • Reduce: feature-parity arms race vs Appstle A places Appstle in Mainstream (low distinctiveness). B1 shows Joy at parity on Features without needing to lead. Don't burn engineering on catalog matching. A · B1
  • Eliminate: Shopify Plus / Hydrogen targeting Leave enterprise to Recharge (87% Plus) and Loop (57% Plus). A places Joy in Boutique Players; C's geo-arbitrage formula doesn't fit enterprise sales motion. A · C
  • Reduce: advanced retention / gamified winback Loop owns this corner (B1 score 10 vs Joy 5). Boutique Players don't have a churn problem yet — chasing Loop dilutes Joy's distinctive position. "Good enough" is the right level. A · B1
  • Reduce: paid acquisition spend C's "trust as compound interest" formula relies on review velocity + Avada cross-sell, not ads. CAC should fall with each cohort, not rise. C
  • Eliminate: tier complexity / multiple plan toggles Boutique Players (per C insight) have "no time for a complex tool." Tier confusion kills the "no value, no charge" message clarity at the moment of decision. C
  • Reduce: US-only marketing focus 84–97% of competitors' merchants are US. Joy's structural advantage is the underserved global SMB segment — over-indexing on US wastes the geo-arbitrage moat. A
Net effect.   Lower CAC (review velocity + Avada cross-sell, not paid ads)  ×  Higher LTV (ecosystem cross-sell + named-partner retention)  ×  Lower cost-to-serve (Vietnam team + systematized playbook)  =  compounding profitability at SMB scale, without the enterprise sales motion or capex of Recharge/Loop.
