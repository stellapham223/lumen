## 2. Audience deep dive

### Primary persona: "The Ambitious Cycle-Curious Woman"
- Female, 24-38
- Knowledge worker, manager, founder, or grad student
- Tracks cycle (Flo, Clue, Apple Health) but feels tracking is data-without-action
- Has read Alisa Vitti's WomanCode OR seen TikTok cycle syncing content
- Skeptical of wellness woo, but also tired of "treat the cycle like it doesn't exist"
- Income: $50K-$150K, US/Canada/UK/Australia primarily
- Search behavior: long-tail informational queries with specific contexts ("luteal phase work meeting", "cycle syncing on birth control")

### Secondary persona: "The Skeptical Researcher"
- Female, 28-45
- Often has medical/STEM background OR has tried wellness fads and burned
- Searches for "is X legit", "does X actually work", "evidence for X"
- High-value reader: shares posts they trust on Twitter/Reddit (link equity)
- Lumen voice already targets this persona well (anti-hype, evidence-based)

### Tertiary persona: "The Symptomatic Searcher"
- Female with PMS, PMDD, endometriosis, PCOS, perimenopause symptoms
- Seeks coping strategies, not lifestyle optimization
- High intent for affiliate products (supplements, trackers, books)
- Need to handle with care: YMYL content (Your Money Your Life, Google's strict E-E-A-T)

### What audience is NOT
- Not pregnant women (different niche, different SERP)
- Not adolescents (different content tone, parental concerns)
- Not "wellness influencer follower" segment (would bounce on Lumen's grounded voice)

## 5. Content gap thesis (what Lumen can OWN)

After SERP analysis across the 16 target keywords, three gaps repeat:

### Gap 1: "Cycle syncing without the woo"
Top SERPs lean heavily on seed cycling, food charts, supplements, with weak evidence backing. Lumen's evidence-graded approach (already in `/blog/is-cycle-syncing-legit`) extends naturally to phase deep-dives.

### Gap 2: "Cycle syncing for knowledge workers, not housewives"
Most cycle syncing content assumes flexible schedule + wellness lifestyle. Lumen targets meeting-heavy professionals: "what to do when you have a 9am presentation in late luteal", "how to handle a sprint deadline mid-menstrual".

### Gap 3: "Cycle syncing under constraints"
Birth control, irregular cycles, perimenopause, PCOS rarely covered with substance. Lumen can answer "if X applies to you, here's what cycle syncing actually means" with grounded analysis.

These three gaps inform the angle of every post in this batch.

## 6. Format strategy per post type

### Standard informational post (1500-2000w)
Structure (template from Test E batch):
1. AI Overview answer paragraph (40-60w, under H1, in `<aside>` Quick takeaway)
2. Bolded thesis sentence in opening section
3. 4-6 H2 sections, each 200-400w
4. Visual element: PhaseGrid, PhaseScheduleCard, or CyclePhasesIllustration
5. FAQ (5-7 Q&A) with FaqJsonLd schema
6. Internal links: 3-5 to other Lumen blog posts + 1-2 to /plan
7. External citations: 3-5 to research papers, authority sites
8. CTA paragraph at end pointing to /plan

### Tier 2 phase deep-dive (2500-3500w)

- 8-10 H2 sections covering: hormonal overview, cognitive characteristics, energy patterns, tasks to schedule, tasks to avoid, workouts, food, social, common questions, when this phase looks different
- 2 visual elements (PhaseGrid for hormones + PhaseScheduleCard for tasks)
- 8-10 FAQ items

- Stronger CTA structure (mid-post + end)

## 7. AI Overview optimization (mandatory per ROADMAP)

Every post MUST have:

1. **40-60 word direct answer paragraph** under H1, wrapped in Quick takeaway aside, with the actionable thesis bolded
2. **H2 matched verbatim to question forms** of target keyword (e.g., "How does cycle syncing work?" not "Mechanism behind cycle syncing")
3. **Inverted pyramid:** answer in first 100 words, supporting detail follows
4. **Multi-question per page:** 5-10 FAQ items covering related searches (each may earn separate AI Overview citation)
5. **FaqJsonLd schema** with each Q&A
6. **One bolded thesis sentence** mid-post (per BLOG_AUDIT_PLAYBOOK rule #8)

This is non-negotiable. AI Overviews appear on 58% of 2026 queries. Without these elements, the post may rank but won't get cited.

## 8. E-E-A-T considerations (YMYL caution)

Women's health is YMYL (Your Money Your Life). Google holds it to higher E-E-A-T standards. Mitigations baked into every post in this batch:

- **Cite peer-reviewed research** for any health claim (PubMed, NCBI, Cochrane, JAMA preferred). Not Wikipedia, not influencer blogs.
- **Acknowledge uncertainty:** "research suggests", "evidence is mixed", "individual variation is large". Hedge language is a positive E-E-A-T signal in YMYL.
- **Author byline (when ready):** Phương's bio in Sprint 4. For now, attribute to "Lumen editorial".
- **Date stamp every post** with "Last reviewed: YYYY-MM-DD" + datePublished in JSON-LD.
- **Disclaimers:** every post mentioning hormones, contraception, or symptoms includes "this is informational, not medical advice. Talk to your provider if [specific signal]."
- **Link to external authority sources** (Mayo Clinic, NIH, ACOG) at least once per post. Counter-intuitive but boosts E-E-A-T because Google reads outbound authority links.

## 9. Internal linking architecture

### Hub-and-spoke design
- **Hubs:** 4 phase deep-dives (Tier 2 posts #12-15). Each hub links to all related cluster posts.
- **Spokes:** All other posts link to relevant phase hub when phase mentioned.
- **Calculator (/plan):** every post links to it once minimum, in CTA.

### Link inventory (after this batch ships, 21 total posts)
- Each phase hub gets 4-6 inbound links from cluster posts
- Each Test E post (5 existing) gets 2-3 new inbound links from new posts
- /plan calculator gets +16 inbound links
- /methodology gets +5 inbound links from posts citing methodology

### Anchor text rules
- Use varied anchor text. NEVER repeat exact-match anchor across multiple links.
- Mix: keyword-match (1x), partial-match (2x), branded ("see Lumen's guide on X"), descriptive ("we compared 6 apps here").
