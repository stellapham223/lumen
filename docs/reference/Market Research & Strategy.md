## 4. Customer Decision Journey (CD Map)

McKinsey 4-phase model adapted to cycle-aware productivity tool:

```
┌────────────┐    ┌────────────┐    ┌────────────┐    ┌────────────┐
│  TRIGGER   │ →  │ EVALUATE   │ →  │   DECIDE   │ →  │  EXPERIENCE │
│            │    │            │    │            │    │  + LOYALTY  │
└────────────┘    └────────────┘    └────────────┘    └────────────┘
```

### Stage 1 — Trigger (problem awareness)

**What happens**: Anya notices inconsistent productivity across the month. Reads a TikTok/Instagram post about cycle syncing OR a colleague mentions it OR she searches "why am I exhausted before my period"

**Search queries** (priority for SEO):
- "why am I tired during luteal phase"
- "PMS brain fog work"
- "cycle syncing productivity"
- "best time to schedule meetings cycle"
- "menstrual phase work deep focus"

**Cycle-Flow opportunity**: Capture via SEO long-tail + programmatic pages.

### Stage 2 — Evaluate (research solutions)

**What happens**: Anya researches options. Reads 3-5 articles. Looks at app reviews. Compares free vs paid.

**Where**: Google search, App Store, Reddit (r/cyclesyncing, r/xxfitness, r/productivity), TikTok creators

**Decision factors** (in order):
1. Free vs paid (huge filter — 60% drop off paid)
2. Web vs app (web wins for "try before commit")
3. Science backing vs woo-woo
4. Productivity focus vs health focus
5. Privacy

**Cycle-Flow advantage**: We win on factors 1, 2, 3, 4 vs apps. Lose on brand recognition.

### Stage 3 — Decide (commitment)

**What happens**: Either downloads app + signs up, OR bookmarks website + tries tool.

**Friction points**:
- App: install (10s) + signup (60s) + permissions (30s) + onboarding (5min) = HIGH
- Cycle-Flow: enter dates (15s) = LOW

**Cycle-Flow advantage**: 20x lower commitment friction → higher conversion at decision stage.

### Stage 4 — Experience + Loyalty (use + advocate)

**What happens**: Uses tool weekly/monthly. Either becomes habit (ideal) or churns within 2 weeks (typical).

**Loyalty drivers**:
- Tool actually changes their behavior (does it work?)
- Email reminders re-engage
- Sharing/social proof
- New content drops keep them coming back

**Cycle-Flow plan**:
- Email list with monthly updates
- Programmatic SEO pages for re-search
- Content articles to deepen engagement
- Multi-tool network (Year 2+) for cross-promotion

| Force | Intensity | Reasoning | Defense |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Threat of new entrants** | **HIGH** | Anyone with AI can build a calculator in a weekend | SEO authority compounds → 1-2 year head start = real moat |

| **Industry rivalry** | **MEDIUM** | Fragmented but growing; no dominant player in our exact niche | Position narrowly (free + web + ambitious women) — don't compete head-on with Phase/Wild.AI |

### Net assessment

**Low-defensibility business in raw form** → moat must be built deliberately:
1. **SEO authority** (long-term, compounds)
2. **Email list** (medium-term, owned channel)
3. **Content depth** (compounds, hard to copy quickly)
4. **Brand association** (Year 2+, "the cycle productivity tool" recall)
5. **Multi-tool network effect** (Year 2+, cross-promote)

→ **Year 1 strategy**: ship + content + SEO compound. Don't try to "defend" before having traffic.

## 15. Pricing & Monetization Strategy

### Why FREE is strategic, not default

Going free is not a "couldn't charge" decision. It's:
1. **SEO requires it** — paywalls = 0 indexing
2. **Counter-positioning** — apps can't follow without self-cannibalizing
3. **Volume play** — ad model needs 10k+ MAU to matter; subscription needs 1k paying users
4. **Trust** — privacy-first + free = no extraction signal

### Revenue stack (priority order)

| Stream | Year 1 estimate | Year 2 estimate | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Programmatic ads (Ezoic → AdSense) | $300-800/mo | $1k-3k/mo | Linear with traffic |
| Affiliate (apps, books, supplements) | $50-200/mo | $200-1k/mo | Email list multiplies |
| Newsletter sponsorships | $0 | $200-1k/mo | Once list >5k |
| Premium PDF guides (one-time) | $0 | $100-500/mo | Year 2 experiment |
| Brand partnerships (femtech) | $0 | $0-500/mo | Year 2 if brand recognized |

### What we will NOT monetize

- ❌ User data (privacy = positioning)
- ❌ Subscription paywall (kills SEO + counter-positioning)
- ❌ Sketchy supplements / fertility woo (brand integrity)
- ❌ Lead-gen for unrelated services (audience trust)

### Cost structure (target Year 1)

| Cost | Monthly | Annual |
|---|---|---|
| Vercel hosting | $0 (free tier) | $0 |
| Domain | $1 | $12 |
| Plausible analytics | $9 | $108 |
| Buttondown email | $9 | $108 |

| **Total** | **$24** | **~$288** |

**Break-even**: Month 4-6 at conservative trajectory.
