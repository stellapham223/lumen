Key Takeaways · three sentences to remember

Three sentences to remember

If a future Lumen collaborator can only remember three things from this map, these are them.

  • 01

    Lumen is Unconventional by design, not by accident

    High distinctiveness (8.0), near-zero centrality (2.0). Free + web + productivity-first is a structural choice subscription incumbents cannot copy without cannibalizing their MRR.

  • 02

    Climb to Aspirational via the X-axis

    Do not change the position, amplify it. SEO authority (120 programmatic pages), email list, and Reddit credibility are the three accelerators on Centrality.

  • 03

    Phase is the closest neighbor in a different lane

    Both Unconventional, but Phase owns paid-app subscribers in fitness-adjacent productivity (5★ review base of 5 raters, all early adopters). Lumen owns free SEO discovery for skeptical knowledge workers. Do not cross lanes.

Eliminate / Reduce
Stop or scale back
  • Eliminate: subscription / paywall monetization Kills SEO and counter-positioning in one move. Every paid competitor's 2025 paywall escalation (Clue Plus, Flo Premium, Natural Cycles 2× price hike) gave away the customer-trust ground Lumen now occupies. A · B1 · B2
